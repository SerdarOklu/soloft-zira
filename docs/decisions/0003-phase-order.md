# 0003: Phases and their order

**Status:** Accepted, 14 November 2026.

## Decision

Development runs in the eight phases listed in [../phases.md](../phases.md). Each phase is
one branch and one pull request, and the next phase starts only after approval.

Two differences from PDT section 13.2: the Water Vault is built before Field Memory, and
deployment to the VPS is a phase of its own. The reasons are in `phases.md`.

## Order

Product order, 0 to 7 (decided by the product owner, 14 November 2026). The faster
submission order (0, 3, 4, 6 first) was considered and not taken.
